Air resistance Honors

Air pushes against the ball's motion, harder the faster it goes (drag grows with v2). Terminal speed is the fastest the ball could ever fall, so a lower terminal speed means the air matters more. The dashed gray path is the same launch with no air.

Watch the components

Turn on Arrows at every dot and launch at 50°.

Which arrow keeps the same length and which one changes? What does that tell you about ax and ay?

Same distance, two angles

Click Level ground, then launch at 30° and again at 60° with the same speed.

What do the two paths share? Find another pair of angles that does the same thing. What is the rule?

Top of the path

Launch, then drag the time slider to the highest point.

What is vy there? Is the ball's speed zero at the top? Check the graphs.

Launch from a height

Set the launch height to 30 m and try angles between 30° and 50°.

Is 45° still the angle that goes the farthest? Why would a higher launch change the best angle?

Add air Honors extension

Open Air resistance, turn it on with a baseball, and launch at 45° and 30 m/s from level ground.

Is the path still symmetric? Which way does ax point, and why? Is 45° still the best angle?
