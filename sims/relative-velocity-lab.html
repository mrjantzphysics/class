Does the current slow the crossing?

Click Aim straight across, launch, then drag the current from 1 m/s to 5 m/s while the boat is moving.

What happens to the time to cross? Which component of the velocity sets that time?

Land straight across

Turn the boat upstream until the landing spot is right across from the start dock. Then check your angle with Land straight across.

Show that sin θ = vwater, ground ÷ vboat, water. Why does it take longer than aiming straight across?

Current faster than the boat

Set the current to 4.0 m/s and the boat to 2.0 m/s.

Can the boat land straight across now? Try different headings and find the one that drifts the least.

Ride the raft

Launch, then switch the view to a raft on the water.

What path does the boat follow in this view? Which of the three velocities is that path showing?

Point the nose at the airport

Use a crosswind, click Point the nose at the airport, and take off.

Where does the plane end up? Compare where the nose points with the direction the plane actually moves.

Correct for the wind

Find a heading that reaches the airport before you press Correct for the wind.

Which way does the nose point compared with the path? Use the vector diagram to explain why.

Headwind and tailwind

Try a 100 km/h headwind, then a 100 km/h tailwind, at the same airspeed.

Which flight is shorter? For a round trip, does the wind help, hurt, or cancel out?

Ride the balloon

Take off in a crosswind, then switch to a balloon in the wind.

Relative to the clouds, which way does the plane move? Why does the airport drift away from where it started?
